# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

This policy covers the Callings Chrome extension and its companion web page
(together, "the app").

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any of your information. It
has no server, no database, no analytics and no tracking. It is only used to
display data from, and save data to, the Google Sheet that you and the people
you work with have shared with each other.

## What the app does

- It shows the contents of the Google Sheet you choose, alongside the
  callings on the Leader and Clerk Resources Organizations page.
- When you make a change, it writes that change to the same Google Sheet.

All of this happens in your own browser. Data goes directly between your
browser and the Google Sheet (through Google's Sheets API) and Leader and
Clerk Resources, using your own signed-in accounts. None of it passes through,
or is kept by, the app's developer or any other third party.

## Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive, store or have access to:

- your name, email address or Google account
- the contents of your Google Sheet
- anything from Leader and Clerk Resources
- your usage of the app

## Google sign-in

The app asks you to sign in with Google only so it can read and write the
Google Sheet you chose. The sign-in token stays in your browser, is used only
to talk to Google's Sheets API, and is never sent anywhere else. You can
revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account's
[third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) settings.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Settings kept in your browser

So you don't have to enter it each time, your browser remembers the link to
the sheet you chose and your light or dark display preference. These stay in
your browser (and, for the extension, your Chrome profile's sync storage) and
are never sent to the developer. Removing the extension or clearing your
browser's site data deletes them.

## Your data in Google Sheets

Everything the app saves lives in your Google Sheet, which you and the people
you share it with own and control. Who can see it is decided by the sheet's
sharing settings in Google Drive, and it is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
